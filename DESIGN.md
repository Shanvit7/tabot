# Tabot operational UI

`/home` is the primary local workspace. One visual at a time: observed context↔site and memory↔context connections on Home; recorded moments over time on Activity; separate observed periods per recurring pattern on Memories. Charts use TanStack Charts. Color is not the only encoding; selectors and text evidence provide keyboard access and exact values. White canvas, ink/green neutrals, restrained lime for recurrence. Manual JSONL export is collapsed by default. `/home?context=<id>` opens one exact context. No legacy route remains.

Landing retains the original neobrutalist brand: lime #bfff00, black/white, self-hosted IBM Plex, square borders and hard offset shadows. Plain browsing-activity copy replaces pipeline jargon. Hero type is capped at 52px, section headings at 36px, and body at 16px. Two Remotion previews reproduce the current 400×600 extension popup (site connections and its real ChatGPT banner), not fictional timelines or sharing interfaces. Previews stay at most 400px wide, use the popup's actual palette and type scale, and disclose sample sites in a small caption. Prerendered static views, reduced-motion support, external pause controls and viewport-gated loading remain. Privacy facts distinguish local activity from authorized ChatGPT summaries leaving the device. See `.ulpi/design/landing.md`. The privacy route retains its original visual language. Extension popup remains separate. Operational CSS: `apps/web/src/styles/work.css`. See `.ulpi/design/work-trace-home.md` for graph relationship rules.
